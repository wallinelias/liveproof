2. Choose 9 cards. If asked, choose "Preserve text" (do not condense).
3. Paste everything between the two lines below.

---

Create a 9-slide pitch deck for "LiveProof", a hackathon project for the City of Espoo challenge "Rethinking Recruitment". Style: modern, airy, public-sector-friendly. Palette: deep royal blue #1F4DB5 (headings, buttons), bright sky blue #5BA4F5 (accents, soft shapes), navy #10244F (text), white background. Big type, one idea per slide, very few words, no stock photos of handshakes, generous whitespace, simple icons. Where I write [SCREENSHOT: ...] leave a clean phone-shaped placeholder frame.

1. Title: "LiveProof" / "Hackathons show who people really are. LiveProof brings that into every recruiting event." / City of Espoo, Rethinking Recruitment.

2. "Your CV was written by AI. So was the screening." / Companies drown in polished, similar applications / Talent struggles to prove real ability / Profiling tools add bias risk.

3. "What if the proof was the work?" / At a recruiting event, companies post a short real problem from the job / Talent solves it live on their phone, solo or in pairs / Same time and tools for everyone. [SCREENSHOT: problem board]

4. "How it works" / 1. Solve live 2. Reviewed blind, in random order 3. Talent chooses when to reveal 4. Humans decide. Show as a simple 4-step horizontal flow.

5. "Talent: show your skills, not your polish" / Skill tags from the problem you solved, and a short note in your own words / Optional peer endorsements from pair rounds / Solo path always fully equal. [SCREENSHOT: showcase]

6. "Company: judge the work, not the CV" / Anonymous solver cards, shown in random order / No name, school or nationality / "I'm interested" is a human action. [SCREENSHOT: blind submissions]

7. "The reveal: one tap, your choice" / Identity stays hidden until the talent reveals / Reversible until contact / Then a mutual contact card and a follow-up note. [SCREENSHOT: reveal and contact card]

8. "Responsible by design" / AI drafts company problems only. It never describes, scores or judges people. / No scoring, no ranking, no match percentages / Random order, blind review / Synthetic data, minimal personal data / Designed to stay outside high-risk AI uses under the EU AI Act.

9. "Try it" / Live demo: https://liveproof-ten.vercel.app/ / Source: https://github.com/wallinelias/liveproof / Closing line: "Hackathons show who people really are. LiveProof brings that into every recruiting event."
